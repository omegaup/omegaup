# AI Contribution Policy

This document describes how contributors can responsibly use AI tools when contributing to omegaUp.

AI tools — such as code assistants, language models, and translation services — can be helpful for coding, documentation, translations, proofreading, research, and other contribution-related tasks. Contributors are welcome to use them. This policy exists to set clear expectations around quality, accountability, and transparency, not to discourage their use.

---

## Responsible Use of AI

- AI should be treated as an assistance tool, not as a replacement for your own judgment or responsibility.
- Use AI where it is useful for the task, rather than relying on it unnecessarily.
- Review and understand any AI-generated or AI-assisted content before submitting it.
- AI output can be incorrect, incomplete, insecure, or inconsistent with the project's conventions. Always verify what it produces.

---

## Contributor Responsibility

Whether or not you use AI, you are fully responsible for the code, documentation, issue reports, pull requests, and other content you submit.

- Verify that your submissions are accurate, relevant, secure, maintainable, and consistent with omegaUp's [coding guidelines](frontend/www/docs/Coding-guidelines.md) and [contribution workflow](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Be prepared to explain the important parts of your changes during review.
- Understand and test your changes before requesting review.

---

## Issues and Communication

- AI may be used to fix typos, translate text, or make communication clearer.
- Before reporting a bug or requesting a feature, explore the platform, check whether the issue is already known, and make sure the report accurately describes the problem or use case.
- For bug reports, verify that the problem is reproducible when possible and provide the steps and relevant details.
- For feature requests, explain the problem or use case clearly and describe why the requested change would be useful.
- Issue descriptions, pull request descriptions, and comments should accurately represent the situation, whether they were written manually or with AI assistance.
- Reports or requests prepared with AI assistance require the same human verification and understanding as those written manually.

---

## Pull Requests

- AI-assisted changes must receive the same level of human review, testing, and validation as manually written changes.
- Provide enough context and evidence for maintainers to evaluate the change.
- Follow the existing [pull request process](CONTRIBUTING.md#pull-request-process) and ensure CI checks pass before requesting review.

---

## Testing and Evidence

These expectations apply to all contributions, regardless of whether AI was used:

- For UI changes, include a screenshot or a short video when useful.
- For backend or behavior changes, provide reproducible steps, tests, benchmarks, logs, or other relevant evidence.
- Add or update tests for new or modified functionality when required by the project's [coding guidelines](frontend/www/docs/Coding-guidelines.md).

For more details, see the [pull request template](PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.MD).

---

## Transparency and Disclosure

- If AI made a significant contribution to your submission — for example, generating a substantial part of a function, proposing an architectural approach, or drafting significant documentation — mention it in your pull request description or a relevant commit message.
- You do not need to disclose trivial uses such as fixing spelling or punctuation, correcting minor typos, small wording improvements, or simple translation and proofreading assistance.
- There is no mandatory format. A short note is enough, for example:

  > Parts of this change were prepared with help from [tool name].

  The exact format can be agreed with maintainers during review.

---

## General Expectations

The core expectations for all contributions are the same whether or not AI is involved: accuracy, understanding, testing, accountability, and useful evidence. These apply to every contributor equally.

Using AI does not lower the quality bar, but it does not raise it either. A good contribution is a good contribution regardless of how it was produced.
